## 主要结果

From August 2022 to February 2023, 120 patients were randomized to receive either GR1802 150 mg (n = 40), GR1802 300 mg (n = 40), or placebo (n = 40), with 107 completing the study.

At Week 16, EASI-75 response rates were 52.5% for GR1802 150 mg, 75.0% for GR1802 300 mg, and 32.5% for placebo. The observed difference for GR1802 300 mg versus placebo was 39.1 percentage points (95% CI 20.0-58.2; p = 0.0002).
